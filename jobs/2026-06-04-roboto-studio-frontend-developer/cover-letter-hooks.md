Roboto’s Next.js + Tailwind + component-library stack matches how I already work: **Next.js** and **App Router** at Tempo and ZeroComplex, **Tailwind** across Codygo and client projects, and **Shadcn/Radix** on personal and contract codebases. At Codygo I spent significant time on a **Chakra design system**—shared buttons, inputs, typography, and Figma-aligned campaign UI—not one-off patches. That matches Roboto’s boutique mindset: unique client solutions built from reusable, well-tested primitives (**Storybook**, integration tests).

## Hook 3 — Figma to production

My Addressable work includes shipping features **according to Figma**—typography, colors, validation, and launch flows—so designers see their intent in production without drift. I’m comfortable in **Figma dev mode** for spacing, tokens, and handoff details.

## Hook 4 — Animation & polish

On my **portfolio** I used **GSAP** for navigation and section motion; on enterprise products I use animation sparingly where it improves clarity (charts, transitions). Happy to grow into Framer Motion/Rive on client work.

## Hook 5 — AI-native workflow

I use **Cursor** daily for refactors, tests, and faster iteration—aligned with Roboto’s appetite for bleeding-edge tooling without sacrificing review quality or craft.

## Hook 6 — Trial period

The **6-month paid trial** makes sense to me: I’d rather prove fit on real Roboto projects than oversell on paper. I’m remote in Lagos, used to async collaboration with design-led teams in other time zones.
